The five strategic moves behind the product
1. Workflow-first, persona workspaces. Every system lists the roles that use it and
what each does. A buyer sees their own staff in the product.
2. One data spine and one learner record. The Enterprise page says a single learner
record follows the student from enquiry to alumnus, with data entered once.
3. Configuration, not code. W orkflows, roles, fees and approvals are configured "in
minutes"
. This is how CampX makes the 8-week promise believable.
4. AI as governance, not a chatbot. Medha inherits the caller's role and tenant and logs
every prompt, retrieval and action. CampX sells AI to cautious leadership by making it
auditable.
5. Trust and content as the sales motion. ISO 27001, the L TI certification and a large
article library aimed at IQAC and leadership do the selling before a demo.
