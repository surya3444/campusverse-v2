15. India-specific compliance and regulatory requirements
T wo legal deadlines shape the build:
DPDP Rules: 13 May 2027. Consent, notice, security and children's-data duties start
then.
Labour Codes: already in force since 21 November 2025. P ayroll must already
comply .
Most other items are regulator directives, board rules or accreditation frameworks. They
bind only certain institution types, or matter because funding, autonomy or rankings
depend on them. This section summarises public sources and is not legal advice. Have
Indian counsel confirm the privacy and labour items before launch.
Types used below:
Law: an Act, Rules or Code. Binding on everyone covered.
Regulator rule: UGC, AICTE or MoE regulation or directive. Binding on the institutions
that regulator covers.
Board rule: binding on schools affiliated to that board.

Accreditation or ranking: formally voluntary , but tied to grants, autonomy or
reputation.
Data system: a government database institutions submit to.
Framework or standard: guidance or a technical standard Campusverse chooses to
meet.

15.1 A compliance architecture that can change with policy
Regulation changes every year , so compliance must be data, not code.
1. Obligation register. Each row in the table above becomes an obligation with an owner
module, the institution types it applies to, its evidence and its deadline.
2. Rule packs. Each board, university and regulator publishes versioned rules with
effective dates and test cases taken from the rulebook. A new circular means a new
pack version, tested, then switched on per institution. Old batches keep their version.
3. Evidence store. Every number filed or reported links back to the records and files it
came from.
4. Mitra Watch. Runs each obligation's check nightly and opens a task when something
is missing.
5. Change process. W atch the sources (UGC, AICTE, CBSE, CISCE, state boards, MeitY ,
Labour) → draft the pack change → legal or academic review → test → release notes →
institutions opt in by date.
Times to plan around:
Consent Manager rules: from mid-November 2026.
