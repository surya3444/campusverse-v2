16. Data flow and system integration
Each piece of data has exactly one owning system. Others read it through that system's
API or react to its events, and never keep their own copy . Reports and Mitra read a
separate reporting store fed by those events. The diagram shows the main hand-offs; the
seven workflows after it give every step.
System interaction · main hand-offs between the nine systems
Read the middle row left to right as a student's journey . Finance, Operations and People
feed it from below , and Health Connect passes only a status. Quality & Compliance and
the reporting store read everything without writing back.
