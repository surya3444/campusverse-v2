17. Prioritised development roadmap
Build in this order:
1. P0: the foundations that every later feature depends on, or that carry legal risk.
2. P1: what a college or university buyer checks before signing.
3. P2: where Campusverse pulls ahead.
4. P3: later bets.
Nothing is on this list only because CampX has it. FFCS, proctoring and perpetual licences
are deliberately late.
Complexity: S = weeks, M = 1–2 months, L = a quarter or more, for a small team.

17.5 Phases and gates
The phases keep the dates of the earlier Rebuild Blueprint. The drawn roadmap is below .
Build roadmap · 5 phases, 5 gates
Phase 0, design and freeze (Nov 2026–Jan 2027). Items 1, 2; V2 domain model, rule-
pack schema, API contracts, design system. Gate: architecture review and counsel's
approval of the consent design.
Phase 1, core platform (Jan–Apr 2027). Items 3–7, 10, 11, 16. Gate: consent and
guardian verification live before 13 May 2027.
<<PAGE 133>>
Phase 2, systems to parity (Apr–Jul 2027). Items 8, 9, 12–15; pilots with one school
group and one college. Gate: parity checklist complete; first V1 tenants cut over at the
June–July boundary .
Phase 3, compliance and depth (Aug–Oct 2027). P1 items 17–36. Gate: all V1 tenants
migrated; V1 retired.
Phase 4, agents (Nov 2027–Jan 2028). First P2 items (37–39, 44, 47). Gate: NIRF data
pack ready for the January 2028 window .
With today's team, expect Phases 2–4 to take about twice as long. Protect the May 2027
gate above everything else.
