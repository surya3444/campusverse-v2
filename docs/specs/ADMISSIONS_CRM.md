# Admissions CRM and verification: spec

Status: **built** (20 November 2026, owner's brief: "Admission should be a module… pipeline, leads, application, follow-ups and bulk upload… forms sendable to an email or a number… verification flow… after he enrolled, every data should be coming under his student profile").

Rule book: §13.3 Enrollment (Admissions CRM: leads and sources, pipeline and follow-ups, bulk import and duplicate check; Applications: multi-step forms, save and resume, documents check by a verification officer; Offers & Onboarding), §16.2 (admission to student record), §15 (DPDP: consent at entry, sensitive data).

## Decisions (_Default_)

1. **Sending.** No SMS, WhatsApp or email provider is connected. A personal link comes back as a ready message that opens in the office's own WhatsApp (`wa.me`), messages (`sms:`) or email (`mailto:`) app, or is copied. Campusverse records when it was sent, opened and submitted. When providers are connected, the same links go through them.
2. **Identity.** Every family proves a mobile with a code before anything is filled in. With a link for an application, it must be a mobile the school has for it. The session lasts two hours; papers uploaded in it carry a signature tying them to that mobile and form.
3. **Verification.** Required papers come from the form. Each is accepted or rejected with a reason; a newer copy replaces the older. Verifying needs every required paper accepted and moves the application to review. The office can still move an application to review by hand ("Other actions").
4. **No duplicates.** A personal link for a lead completes that lead. Office leads, bulk uploads and public applications refuse the same mobile and child in the same year.
5. **Sensitive data.** Religion, category and health notes are optional, marked as such on the form, written with sensitive audit rows, and go only to the student record (never the owner's dashboard).
6. **Enrolment carries everything.** Background, address, previous school, health notes, the second parent with parent access, both emails, the school's own questions (same key and type), the photo and the current copy of each paper with its check.
7. **Undoing a bulk upload** closes untouched leads as lost; history is never deleted.
