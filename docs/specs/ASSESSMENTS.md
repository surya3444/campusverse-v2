# Assessments

Academics › Assessments (rule book §13.2; P1 item 16). Question Bank and Quizzes & Tests are for
every profile; the CIE plan (weights from the regulation) and the gradebook with locks are built in
academics/exams for colleges and universities.

- **Question bank** (`assessment.bank.manage`: teachers and leaders): multiple-choice (2–6 options,
  one correct), short and long questions per subject and topic, with marks, difficulty, a Bloom
  level (revised taxonomy) and, in HE, the CO code it measures. Archiving takes a question out of
  the bank without touching papers that use it.
- **Question papers**: a title, subject, time and instructions; questions picked from the bank in
  order. Each is copied onto the paper when added, so later bank edits never change it. The
  blueprint shows marks by Bloom level, difficulty and CO, the share at "apply" or above, and how
  many questions have no CO. Finalising freezes the paper (a database trigger refuses changes).
  Printed with or without the answer key.

Not yet: online tests with auto-marking (needs student sign-in in the student app), rubrics for
long answers, sharing a bank across a group's institutions, importing questions from a file.
