# Grievances & Committees

Quality & Compliance › Grievances & Committees (rule book §13.11, §15; P1 item 33). Higher
education first (UGC anti-ragging, student grievance redressal, SC/ST cell); the POSH internal
committee applies to every employer with ten or more staff, so schools get it too.

## Who and what

- **Leaders** (`grievance.committee.manage`): set up committees (kind, name, days to decide) and
  their members: staff of the institution as chair or member, and named outside members (the POSH
  external member). They see each committee's counts, never a case.
- **Anyone** (`grievance.case.raise`; parents by the guardian relation, about their own child):
  raises a case with a subject and details, optionally anonymous to the committee; follows it in
  "My cases"; appeals a decision within 30 days.
- **Committee members**: see and work only their committee's cases: notes (append-only), an
  inquiry, a decision with an outcome, closing; record meetings with minutes.

## Rules

1. Cases are numbered per committee. Due dates come from the committee's days (defaults: POSH 90,
   anti-ragging 7, grievance redressal 15, others 30; counsel confirms).
2. Neither the subject nor the details go into the audit log or any message.
3. Moves: received → inquiry → decided → closed; decided → appealed (raiser only, 30 days) → closed.
