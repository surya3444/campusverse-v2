# Feedback & Surveys

Academics › Feedback & Surveys (rule book §13.4; P1 item 18). Every profile.

- **Survey builder** (`survey.form.manage`): a survey starts from a template (course, faculty,
  facility, parent, exit, alumni, employer, other) with rating questions (1–5, each with a weight),
  choice and text questions; questions change only while it is a draft.
- **Cycles and responses**: open for dates to one audience (staff, parents, students); each person
  answers once. Anonymous by default: who answered is kept apart from the answers, with no time or
  ordered id to link them. Every rating question must be answered.
- **Results**: average per question, counts per rating or option, comments, and the weighted
  score Σ(average × weight) / Σweight out of 5.
- **Action taken**: actions with a staff owner and due date, marked done with what was done:
  the evidence NAAC asks for.
