# academics/exams

Academics › Exams & Results for colleges and universities (rule book §13.2 Assessments, Exams & Results; P0 item 12). Schools keep school/exams.

- **Marks** (`marksSheet`, `saveMarks`; `academics.exam.marks`): internal (CIE) and end-exam (SEE) marks per course of a batch's current term, out of the grading scale's split (built in: 40 and 60). The students of a course are those whose approved course registration includes it, or everyone for the term's compulsory courses when a student has no approved registration (academics/registration `approvedCourses`). Absent from the end exam is recorded as such.
- **Internal assessment components** (rule book §13.2 "CIE Plan", "Gradebook"): when the regulation's course has a plan (academics/structure `cieComponents`), the marks sheet takes a score per component (`exam_cie_score`) and the internal mark is worked out (`cieFrom`: each score's share of its maximum, weighted, scaled to the internal maximum, two decimals); a typed internal mark is refused for such a course.
- **Locks** (`setCourseLock`; `academics.exam.manage`): a course's marks are locked only when every student's are complete; nobody changes locked marks; reopening is refused once results are sent.
- **Results** (`examBoard`, `sendResults`, `decideResults`): `termResults` grades each course on the credits pack's scale (`packages/rules` `courseGrade`: the total's band, or F when the end exam is below its pass mark or missed), SGPA weighted by credits and CGPA with earlier published terms (`gradePointAverage`). Sending freezes the grades, SGPA and CGPA (append-only) with the grading scale used, and starts an `academics.results` approval in the workflow engine (default: `academics.exam.publish`, sensitive; never the sender). Students see nothing until published.
- **Re-exams** (`requestReexam`, `markReexam`, `decideReexam`, `reexamBoard`; `domain/reexam.ts`): supplementary for a failed course (internal marks carry over, the new end exam stands) and revaluation of an end-exam script (counts only if it does better; a grade never goes down). Registered by the exam office (`academics.exam.manage`), marked (`academics.exam.marks`) on the term's frozen scale, approved through the workflow engine (`academics.reexam`; never the marker). Approved outcomes override the published grade wherever it is read (`effectiveTerms`: grade cards, CGPA of later terms, the ABC file); SGPA and CGPA are worked out again for those students only; the frozen `exam_grade` rows never change.
- **Hall tickets & eligibility** (`eligibilityBoard`, `issueHallTickets`, `hallTicketUrl`, `sendIneligibilityNotices`; `domain/eligibility.ts`; rule book §13.2, §16): for the current term, a course may be sat when the student's attendance (academics/attendance `courseStandings`) is eligible or condoned, or none was taken yet; overdue fees (finance `overdueOf`) hold the whole ticket back unless the exam office releases it with a reason (audited). A ticket is a frozen, QR-verifiable document (platform/documents, kind `hall_ticket`) listing the courses the student may sit; it is issued again as a new version only when those courses change (after a condonation, say). Ineligibility notices with the reasons go to the student and their guardians, once per set of reasons.
- **Grade card** (`gradeCard`): a student's published terms with grades (after re-exams), SGPA and CGPA, printable.
- **ABC & NAD** (`abcCreditFile`; rule book §15, P0 item 15): a published term's credits, one row per student per course (`ABC_COLUMNS`), with full ABC and APAAR IDs opened through people/records `learnerIdsForExport`; needs `people.student.reveal` and is audited as a restricted export. Students without an ABC ID are listed. A working file: check the columns against the current NAD template. Shown in Government Reports for colleges and universities.

Anyone who reads the academic structure sees the board, so an approver named in the designer can act; marks and locks keep their own permissions.

**Owns:** `exam_mark`, `exam_course_lock`, `exam_result`, `exam_grade`, `exam_gpa` (migration 0067), `exam_reexam` (migration 0072), `exam_cie_score` (migration 0073), `exam_hall_ticket` (migration 0075). **Events:** `academics.results.published.v1`, `academics.reexam.approved.v1`, `academics.hall_ticket.issued.v1`.

**Not yet:** no-dues checks beyond fees (library, hostel), students seeing their hall ticket in their app, regional-script names on the ticket PDF, seating and invigilation, dual valuation and moderation, improvement exams, re-exam fees and the student's own request from the app, a course's own maximum marks, students and parents seeing results in their apps, digitally signed transcripts, a direct NAD/DigiLocker upload.
