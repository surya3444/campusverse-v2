# Payroll (`@campusverse/people-payroll`)

Staff salaries and the monthly payroll (docs/specs/PAYROLL.md).

- **Owns:** `payroll_salary` (dated salaries), `payroll_run` (one per institution per month; draft, approved, paid), `payroll_slip` (frozen once the run is approved), `payroll_settlement` (migration 0065), `payroll_arrear` (migration 0066), `payroll_tds_challan` (migration 0071).
- **Reads:** staff attendance's `payrollInputsIn` (loss of pay), people/records (staff membership), organisation (the state, for professional tax), `packages/rules` `payrollRules`.
- **Events:** `payroll.run.approved.v1`, `payroll.run.paid.v1`, `payroll.settlement.paid.v1`.
- **Permissions:** `payroll.salary.manage` (sensitive), `payroll.run.prepare`, `payroll.run.approve` (sensitive; never the preparer), `payroll.slip.self`.
- **Domain:** `computeSlip` (prorating, PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, never below zero), whole paise, tested in `domain/pay.test.ts`.
- **Statutory (rule book §13.7, §15):** `statutoryReturns` from an approved or paid run (`payroll.run.prepare` or `.approve`): EPFO's ECR 2.0 text (`ecrMember`: UAN, name, gross, EPF/EPS/EDLI wages capped at the ceiling, employee PF, EPS at `payrollRules().pf.epsBp`, the employer's remainder, NCP days), staff left out for want of a UAN (people/records `statutoryIds`), and the month's PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS totals for the challans. `wageRuleShortfall` flags basic + DA below half of gross (Labour Codes); how PF treats the shortfall is for the payroll adviser. Also the ESIC monthly contribution file (`esiMember`: IP number, name, whole days paid, wages in rupees; staff without an IP number listed) and labour welfare fund totals. **TDS** (`application/tds.ts`, `domain/tds.ts`): `recordTdsChallan`/`deleteTdsChallan` (BSR code, serial, deposit date, amount against a salary month; `payroll.run.prepare`); `tdsQuarter` links each approved or paid slip's TDS to a challan of its month (`linkChallans`), flags late deposits (7th of the next month, March by 30 April: `TDS_SALARY` in packages/rules, unverified) and lists what stops the 24Q (no TAN on the legal entity, unpaid months, uncovered deductions, missing PANs); with `file`, the Annexure I worksheet with full PANs (people/records `pansForExport`, audited as restricted). `form16Figures`: each employee's Part B figures for the year (components, PT, employee PF, TDS by quarter), with a PAN CSV on request. Not yet: the tax computation (regimes, declarations), Annexure II in Q4, the RPU/FVU file itself and TRACES (Part A).
- **Arrears (rule book §13.7 Assignments & Arrears):** when a run is computed, each of the 12 earlier approved months is recomputed on the salary now in force for it, with the same days and loss of pay (`arrearsFor`); the difference from what was paid (its slip plus arrears already paid for it) is added to this month's gross, PF and ESI and recorded in `payroll_arrear`, so it is paid once. Professional tax stays on the month's own salary. A reduction is never recovered automatically.
- **Labour welfare fund:** state packs carry the employee and employer amounts and the months they are collected (built in: December); slips deduct the employee share and record the employer's.
- **Final settlement (rule book §13.7; Labour Codes):** `settlements` lists staff who left in the last 120 days (people/records `staffLeftSince`) with what each is owed (`settlementFor`): the final month's days through `computeSlip` unless an approved run already paid that month, and gratuity (`packages/rules` `gratuity`, rules from the labour pack via `gratuityRulesAt`) on Labour Codes wages — basic + DA plus any wage-rule shortfall — after five years, or one year for fixed-term (`employment_type = 'contract'`) staff. It falls due two working days after the last day (`addWorkingDays`, skipping Sundays and the institution's holidays). `prepareSettlement` records it and starts a `payroll.settlement` approval in the workflow engine (amount-based steps from the designer; never the preparer); `decideSettlement`, then `markSettlementPaid` with a date and bank reference. Overdue and paid-late are shown and audited.
- **Wage rule on salaries:** the salary form warns live when basic + DA is below half of gross (the same rule as `wageRuleShortfall`).
