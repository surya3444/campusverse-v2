# school/timetable

The school timetable (docs/specs/TIMETABLE.md, Milestone 19). School only: college and university timetables come with them. Sections, subjects with periods per week and subject teachers come from school-setup's `teachingPlan`; holidays from school-attendance's `holidayOn`.

| Use case                                                                            | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `listTimetables`                                                                    | Every version with where it stands today: draft, scheduled (from a later date), current, superseded (`school.timetable.read`)                                                                                         |
| `timetableDetail`                                                                   | One version for the editor: bell schedule (with "Period n" counting class periods only), slots, sections, subjects, staff, periods placed per subject against the target                                              |
| `createTimetable`, `updateSchedule`                                                 | A draft with its name, working days and bell schedule (`school.timetable.manage`); lessons on dropped days or rows that became breaks go                                                                              |
| `copyTimetable`                                                                     | A new draft with the same schedule and lessons: how a published timetable changes                                                                                                                                     |
| `setSlot`                                                                           | One cell of a draft: subject from the class's subjects, teacher, room; or clear it. A teacher or room already used then is refused, naming the other section                                                          |
| `publishTimetable`                                                                  | From a date (today or later, in the year). Refused while a shared teacher overlaps another school's published timetable at the same time                                                                              |
| `dayPlan`                                                                           | A day's lessons from the version in effect; none on a holiday or a day off                                                                                                                                            |
| `coverDay`, `assignCover`, `cancelCover`, `freeTeachers`                            | Away teachers' lessons with ranked free teachers; cover by a teacher, combined or supervised; who is free in a period (`school.cover.manage`)                                                                         |
| `saveGroup`, `deleteGroup`, `setGroupSlot`                                          | Teaching groups of a draft: sections that split by subject in a period; placed with each subject's teacher and room, under the same clash rules                                                                       |
| `saveRules`, `requestGeneration`, `generation`, `placeGeneration`, `generationJobs` | Generating under the school's rules (`domain/generate.ts`): the run goes to the worker (`school.timetable.generate_requested.v1`), the result is kept and previewed, Place writes exactly it unless the draft changed |
| `childWeek`                                                                         | A child's lessons today and the coming school days, for their guardian (`school.timetable.read`, guardian relation)                                                                                                   |
| `timetableTools`                                                                    | Verse AI `timetable.today` and `timetable.free_teachers`: the asker's own lessons and counts, no names                                                                                                                |

Rules without a database: `domain/timetable.ts` (bell schedule check, version status, ISO weekday, periods-per-week targets) and `domain/cover.ts` (ranking free teachers).

**Owns:** `school_timetable` (draft or published, effective from, days), `school_timetable_period` (bell schedule), `school_timetable_slot` (a section's or a teaching group's; unique per section, day and period; unique teacher and room per day and period). `school_timetable_group` and `school_timetable_group_section`. `school_timetable_generation` (each run: rules, draft fingerprint, result). `school_cover` (one live cover per lesson and day; one lesson per covering teacher per period; cancelled, never deleted). Published versions are frozen by a trigger. **Events:** `school.timetable.published.v1`, `school.cover.assigned.v1`, `school.timetable.generate_requested.v1`.

Exam days come from school-exams' `papersOn`: a sitting class has no lessons that day.

**Not yet:** separate bell schedules, which student takes which group subject, discarding a draft.
