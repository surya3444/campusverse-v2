# school/timetable

The school timetable (docs/specs/TIMETABLE.md, Milestone 19). School only: college and university timetables come with them. Sections, subjects with periods per week and subject teachers come from school-setup's `teachingPlan`; holidays from school-attendance's `holidayOn`.

**Generator settings** (`Rules`, saved with the draft): fill or rebuild; search effort (quick, standard, thorough) and a seed (another seed, another arrangement). Hard rules: a subject's times a day (school-wide or per subject), double periods, rooms, periods a subject never takes (`avoidPeriods`) and the only days it is taught (`days`); a teacher's days off, unavailable periods and particular times, most periods a day, in a row and in the week (`maxPerWeek`). Preferences: morning or afternoon subjects, the same period every day (`samePeriod`), the class teacher in each section's first period (`classTeacherFirst`, from setup's `teachingPlan`), and teachers' days balanced or compact. The result reports how many of each preference were met. The screen (`generator.tsx`) is one guided flow on the timetable page (owner's brief, 21 Nov 2026), so nobody leaves it: 1 the school day (working days and bell schedule; a new timetable starts here), 2 each class's subjects and periods a week, 3 who teaches each subject to each section with every teacher's weekly load, 4 the rules and preferences below, 5 a check that it fits (a class planned over the week's periods or a teacher over their limit stops it; subjects without periods or a teacher are noted). Steps 2 and 3 save through school-setup's own API (`setOffering`, `assignTeacher`, `addSubject`; `school.setup.manage`), the same data the Subjects page edits. After placing, the draft can be published from a date in the same flow. It follows the hub's generator: settings in four tabs, a working view, then the week per section with new lessons marked, "Try another arrangement" and "Place".

Rules without a database: `domain/timetable.ts` (bell schedule check, version status, ISO weekday, periods-per-week targets) and `domain/cover.ts` (ranking free teachers).

**Owns:** `school_timetable_feed` (calendar links), `school_timetable` (draft or published, effective from, days), `school_timetable_period` (bell schedule), `school_timetable_slot` (a section's or a teaching group's; unique per section, day and period; unique teacher and room per day and period). `school_timetable_group` and `school_timetable_group_section`. `school_timetable_generation` (each run: rules, draft fingerprint, result). `school_cover` (one live cover per lesson and day; one lesson per covering teacher per period; cancelled, never deleted). Published versions are frozen by a trigger. **Events:** `school.timetable.published.v1`, `school.cover.assigned.v1`, `school.timetable.generate_requested.v1`.

Exam days come from school-exams' `papersOn`: a sitting class has no lessons that day.
