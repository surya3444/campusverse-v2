# shared/finance

Fees (Schools Release spec, "Fees"): a parent sees the bill before paying, always gets a complete receipt, and the accountant closes each day without spreadsheets. Money is integer paise everywhere; `domain/money.ts` holds the rules, with property-based tests.

**Years (owner's brief, 20 Nov 2026):** a fee plan bills for one academic year (`fee_plan.period_id`, `period_label`, migration 0091). Finance knows nothing about years itself: it asks `AudiencePort.periods` (school: the school's years). `savePlan` takes `periodId`, or infers it from the first due date; due dates fall in the year, the first up to 180 days early (`due_outside_year`); a plan family keeps its year (`year_fixed`). A closed year refuses new charges (`issuePlan` and positive adjustments: `year_closed`) but still takes payments. Billing a student and the fee disclosure use the plans of the year in question, so this year's class plan doesn't bill next year's class. `feeYears` lists the years for the plan editor.

**Fees collected for others (payees.ts, owner's brief 21 Nov 2026):** part of a fee can belong to an outside body: the affiliating university (VTU's ₹20,000 inside an engineering college's fee), a board, the government. `savePayee` adds the body (`fee_payee`, per institution); a head is the institution's own or collected for one payee (`fee_head.payee_id`, set at `createHead` or by `setHeadPayee` until the head is first billed, `head_in_use` after). Every allocation is split by payee as it is made (`allocateToInvoice` → `splitByPayee`): outside payees first, up to what each is still owed on that invoice, the institution takes the rest, so it never spends money it owes on. Concessions on a payee's head reduce its share; late fees and bounce charges are the institution's. Receipts carry the split (`forOthers`). A reversal takes each share back down. `recordRemittance` records money paid on with its bank or draft reference, never more than is held for that year (`more_than_held`), append-only. `payees` and `payeeStatement` show billed, collected, paid on and held, per year or all, with the student-by-student list the university asks for. Refunds come from the institution's own share. When the general ledger arrives, a payee's share posts to a liability ("payable to VTU") and a remittance clears it.

**Donations (donations.ts):** voluntary gifts to the legal entity, never a fee and never for a seat: a capitation fee is unlawful (RTE Act 2009 s.13; state capitation fee acts). `recordDonation` needs the recorder's voluntary confirmation, a mobile or email for a person, a reference for anything but cash, a date this financial year and not ahead; it is refused while the donor's family has an admission in progress at the institution or a child admitted in the last 90 days (`admissionInProgress` port, school: admissions; `admission_in_progress`). Gapless `DON/<FY>/000001` numbers per legal entity and financial year (`donation_counter`). `donations` lists a year with totals by fund; `donationReceipt` prints with the voluntary statement; `cancelDonation` is by someone other than the recorder, with a reason, and a database trigger allows nothing else to change a donation (no delete). Not yet: 80G certificates (Form 10BE) and Form 10BD with donor PAN, online donations, and fund balances in a ledger.

**Part shares:** when bills are made, finance asks the audience port's optional `shares` for students who pay part of a group's fee (transport: one-way riders pay half the zone fee). Each line is scaled down (rounded down) before it is split into instalments. Bills already made stay as they were.

**Receipts** are numbered per legal entity and financial year (April–March) with no gaps, are append-only (no UPDATE or DELETE grant, plus a trigger), and keep a snapshot of what was printed, including the student's class and admission number on the day (from `deps.placements`, school: `schoolPlacement`) and the balance left. Corrections are credit notes. The counter search finds students by name or admission number (through `findByReference`) and shows class and admission number, so same-name students can be told apart. Dues count past-due instalments only.

**Gateway:** `chooseGateway` picks Razorpay when `RAZORPAY_*` are set, a fake gateway in development (with `POST /api/v1/finance/webhooks/dev-pay/:paymentId` to complete a payment), and none otherwise.

**Owned tables:** `fee_head`, `fee_plan`, `fee_plan_line`, `fee_instalment`, `fee_invoice`, `fee_invoice_line`, `fee_concession`, `fee_payment`, `fee_allocation`, `fee_receipt_counter`, `fee_receipt`, `fee_refund`, `fee_reversal`, `fee_day_close`; `ops.payment_ref`, `ops.webhook_event` (migration 0009); `fee_payee`, `fee_remittance`, `donation_counter`, `donation` (0092).

**Events:** `finance.payment.succeeded.v1` (receipt message), `finance.payment.reversed.v1`, `finance.refund.issued.v1`, `finance.invoice.overdue.v1`, `finance.payment.started.v1` (status checks), `finance.remittance.recorded.v1`, `finance.donation.recorded.v1`, `finance.donation.cancelled.v1`.

**Not yet:** Autopay, bank statement matching, Tally export, written-off workflow, offline counter with provisional slips, refund slab calculation and leaving-student proration screens (`prorate` exists in the domain), state fee-limit rule packs beyond the increase warning, and GST invoices for taxable heads.
