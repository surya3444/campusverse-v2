# shared/finance

Fees (Schools Release spec, "Fees"): a parent sees the bill before paying, always gets a complete receipt, and the accountant closes each day without spreadsheets. Money is integer paise everywhere; `domain/money.ts` holds the rules, with property-based tests.

| Area         | Functions                                                                                                                      | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plans        | `createHead`, `listHeads`, `savePlan`, `publishPlan`, `planDetail`, `listPlans`                                                | Heads carry a tax class (a CA confirms defaults), RTE waiver and refundable flags. Plans are versioned: a fee change mid-year is a new version of the same family                                                                                                                        |
| Invoices     | `previewIssue`, `issueInvoices`, `adjustInvoice`                                                                               | Preview then commit. One invoice per student, plan family and instalment, so issuing twice bills nobody twice. Later joiners aren't billed for instalments already due. Approved concessions apply on issue                                                                              |
| Counter      | `searchStudents`, `studentAccount`, `collectPayment`, `receipt`                                                                | Requires an `Idempotency-Key`: a retry returns the same receipt. Oldest due first; late fees once per invoice; any excess is kept as credit                                                                                                                                              |
| Online       | `startOnlinePayment`, `handleWebhook`, `checkOnlinePayment`, `recheckPayment`, `reconciliation`                                | Razorpay Payment Links. Webhooks verified (HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body), recorded by event id, and applied idempotently; a lost webhook is caught by status checks at 15 min, 1 h and 6 h. A paid amount that differs goes to reconciliation                                           |
| Approvals    | `requestConcession`/`decideConcession`, `requestRefund`/`decideRefund`, `requestReversal`/`decideReversal`, `pendingApprovals` | Maker can't approve their own (checked in code and by a database CHECK). Refunds go through the approval engine (`finance.refund`, amount limits add steps); the last step needs the payout reference and issues a refund voucher; reversals get a credit note and can add a bank charge |
| Day and dues | `dayCloseView`, `closeDay`, `dues`, `previewReminders`, `sendReminders`                                                        | Counted cash against collections; a difference needs a note; a closed day stops that cashier's counter. Dues by age; reminders once per student per day. Dues never cut off app access or safety notices                                                                                 |

**Receipts** are numbered per legal entity and financial year (April–March) with no gaps, are append-only (no UPDATE or DELETE grant, plus a trigger), and keep a snapshot of what was printed, including the student's class and admission number on the day (from `deps.placements`, school: `schoolPlacement`) and the balance left. Corrections are credit notes. The counter search finds students by name or admission number (through `findByReference`) and shows class and admission number, so same-name students can be told apart. Dues count past-due instalments only.

**Gateway:** `chooseGateway` picks Razorpay when `RAZORPAY_*` are set, a fake gateway in development (with `POST /api/v1/finance/webhooks/dev-pay/:paymentId` to complete a payment), and none otherwise.

**Owned tables:** `fee_head`, `fee_plan`, `fee_plan_line`, `fee_instalment`, `fee_invoice`, `fee_invoice_line`, `fee_concession`, `fee_payment`, `fee_allocation`, `fee_receipt_counter`, `fee_receipt`, `fee_refund`, `fee_reversal`, `fee_day_close`; `ops.payment_ref`, `ops.webhook_event` (migration 0009).

**Events:** `finance.payment.succeeded.v1` (receipt message), `finance.payment.reversed.v1`, `finance.refund.issued.v1`, `finance.invoice.overdue.v1`, `finance.payment.started.v1` (status checks).

**Not yet:** Autopay, bank statement matching, Tally export, written-off workflow, offline counter with provisional slips, refund slab calculation and leaving-student proration screens (`prorate` exists in the domain), state fee-limit rule packs beyond the increase warning, and GST invoices for taxable heads.
