# shared/staff-attendance

Staff attendance for any institution type (docs/specs/INSTITUTION_DEPTH.md, Milestone 20; decision 0020).

| Use case                                           | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `checkIn`, `myDay`                                 | Staff check themselves in and out (`staff.attendance.check_in`, a 'self' grant on every staff role), from the web or the app. The device's event id makes an offline retry harmless. Where they were is kept against the campus gate (platform/organisation); off campus is flagged, or refused when the institution requires being on campus                                   |
| `register`, `markDay`                              | The office's day register (`staff.attendance.read` / `.manage`). A mark wins over check-ins; changing a marked day needs a reason. Future days take only leave or duty                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `attendanceSettings`, `updateAttendanceSettings`   | Start and end of day, grace minutes for late and early leaving, the length of a full day, whether check-ins must be on campus                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `monthReport`                                      | A month per staff member: days and totals (`domain/day.ts`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `askLeave`, `leaveQuote`, `myLeave`, `cancelLeave` | Staff leave, on the web and in the app (docs/specs/STAFF_LEAVE.md): ask for one's own (`staff.leave.apply`, self) or, as the office, for someone (`staff.leave.manage`). Days are working days (no Sundays, no school holidays, which come through `HolidaysPort`); the balance must hold them, pending days included. Cancel while pending, or before an approved leave starts |
| `decideLeave`, `leaveRequests`                     | Through the approval engine (`staff.leave`; by default anyone with `staff.leave.approve`, or the designer's steps); nobody decides their own (a database check too). Approving writes each day into the register (`on_leave`, or `half_day`) in the same transaction, so the timetable's cover screen sees the teacher away                                                     |
| `leaveTypes`, `saveLeaveType`, `installLeaveTypes` | Organisation-wide types (CL, SL, EL, LOP at sign-up): paid, days a year, carry forward, half days. Retired, never deleted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `leaveBalances`, `adjustLeave`                     | Balances by leave year (April to March, `domain/leave.ts`): days + carried + adjustments − approved − pending. Adjustments are append-only with a reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `payrollInputs`                                    | A month per staff member: working days, present, leave paid and unpaid, absent, not marked, loss of pay and paid days, for the accountant's payroll tool. No salary figures are kept                                                                                                                                                                                            |

Days are counted in India time. Who is on staff comes from people/records (`staffAt`).

**Verse AI:** `staff.attendance.today`, `staff.leave.today` (counts only).

**Owns:** `staff_attendance_settings`, `staff_attendance_checkin` (append-only), `staff_attendance_day`, `staff_leave_type`, `staff_leave_request`, `staff_leave_adjustment` (append-only). **Events:** `staff.attendance.checked.v1`, `staff.attendance.marked.v1`, `staff.leave.requested.v1`, `staff.leave.decided.v1`, `staff.leave.cancelled.v1`.

**Not yet:** check-in from the Expo app (the API takes `source: 'app'`), biometric or RFID imports, salary runs and payslips (deferred; see the leave spec).
